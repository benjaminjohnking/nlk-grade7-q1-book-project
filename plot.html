Natalie King Grade 7 Q1 Book Project

Plot

Three troubled children, Marcus, Kris and Angie, and their families travel to Gentle Valley, a small town that is host to a popular harvest festival. The children become suspicious as they learn that Halloween themes are forbidden.

While participaing in festival activities, they learn about the legend of The Harvester, a scarecrow that kidnaps children and turns them into pumpkins. The children are horrified to learn that the townspeople are complicit in the kidnappings, trusting The Harvester to protect the town and provide a good harvest in exchange for their sacrifice of the children. The townspeople attract families with troubled children to the harvest festival so they can be lured in for sacrifice.

Marcus, Kris and Angie decide to put a stop to The Harvester, rescuing the kidnapped children and freeing the souls of those who were killed. The freed souls get their revenge on The Harvester, tearing him to pieces. With the curse having been broken, the townspeople of Gentle Valley gradually leave the town.

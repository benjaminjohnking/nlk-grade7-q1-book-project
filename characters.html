Natalie King Grade 7 Q1 Book Project

Characters

The story consists of three protagonists, Marcus, Kris and Angie. The three children are classmates who are known to get in trouble at school. They are not friends at first, but eventually bond as they work together to break the curse and free the souls of the children who were killed.

The story also consists of several antagonists. Liv Thompson is the overly cheerful organizer of the harvest festival who tells the children that Halloween is forbidden from the town. The Harvester is a haunted scarecrow who kidnaps children and turns them into pumpkins. The Townspeople are the residents of Gentle Valley who sacrifice children to the The Harvester.

The story also includes Elena Thompson, who is the daughter of Liv Thompson. When she learns that Marcus, Kris and Angie want to break the curse of The Harvester, she decides to help them by providing clues about where the children are being held captive and what the adults are planning to do with them.

Natalie King Grade 7 Q1 Book Project

Themes

Appearances Can Be Deceiving

The parents brought their children to Gentle Valley, believing that a trip to the harvest festival would be an opportunity to help their troubled children. They did not realize that the townspeople intended to sacrifice their children.

Stay Curious And Ask Questions

The children noticed multiple things about Gentle Valley that were unusual, such as the ban on Halloween and the creepy scarecrow known as The Harvester. Rather than ignoring their concerns, they decided to investigate further, eventually discovering the truth about the town.

Some Traditions Need To Be Broken

Despite child sacrifice being a longtime tradition of Gentle Valley, the children realized that it needed to stop. They felt it was wrong for children to be killed, and were willing to put themselves in harm’s way to break the curse.

People Can Bond Through Teamwork

Despite not being friends when arriving to Gentle Valley, the children made a good team to work together and break the curse. They eventually bonded, promising not to tell their parents and choosing to keep a secret between the three of them.
